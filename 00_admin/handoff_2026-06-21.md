toward DiD):

  (wild bootstrap). The naive analytic p=0.008 is **over-optimistic** — NOT the headline.
- **Synthetic DiD (pooled)** −26.9% — **no valid SE** (5 treated, 5 donors). The earlier z=−7.7 was a
  **donor-jackknife artifact, WITHDRAWN.** Per-commodity SDID placebo z's −1.5..−3.4 (wheat −1.5 insignificant).
- **SCM (Abadie, commodity)** chana −40%, mustard −40%, wheat −49%, soybean −25%, moong **+3% (wrong sign)**;

(STRONGER)** → not wheat/MSP-driven; ex-chana −18.3% the lone marginal case.
