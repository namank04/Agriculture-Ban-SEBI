6. What was lost — price discovery & efficiency (H2, H3)
7. Did the ban work? (H1 volatility, H4 insulation)

9. Policy discussion — stakeholder cost map; what the current SEBI review should weigh
10. Conclusion
Appendices: remaining four commodities; robustness battery; data construction
